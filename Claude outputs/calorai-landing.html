How It Works

From snap to logged in three seconds

No barcodes, no databases to search, no typing. Just point, shoot, and done.

1

Open the camera

Tap the camera button in the app. CalorAI uses your phone's camera to analyse what's on your plate — no separate setup needed.

2

Snap your meal

Take a photo of your food. Our AI model identifies the dish, estimates portions, and calculates calories and macros instantly.

3

Confirm and log

Review the AI's breakdown, adjust the portion if needed, then tap Log. Your daily totals update automatically in real time.
